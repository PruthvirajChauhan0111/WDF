What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a student portal concept designed to bring academic information together in one convenient place. It provides pages for courses, attendance, results, timetables and college events.

Our Features

📚 Course Information

Browse subjects and learn about your academic program.

📊 Attendance Tracking

View example attendance records for different subjects.

🏆 Academic Results

Explore a sample results page and semester performance.

📱 Responsive Design

Access the interface on mobile phones, tablets and desktops.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a simple, accessible and modern interface that makes academic information easy to navigate.
